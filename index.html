Completion improves monotonically up to roughly 500–1,000 and then reverses — the highest threshold has the best completion rate and still loses money, plausibly adverse selection, since the busiest markets are where an informed counterparty is most likely to be the one lifting us. Regimes are different strategies, not tweaks — pooling them once produced a “today’s maker result” that blended three configurations logged hours apart. Each book carries the regime stamped at post time, so a mixed day cannot silently average them. Compare within a regime, never across.
A complete book earns the overround and is outcome-independent; a stranded one is a naked position on whichever side the market is moving toward — which is why 2-of-3 is the worst state, not 1-of-3. The gate quotes only books liquid enough to finish: on this history, min leg volume ≥500 lifted completion 36% → 60% and turned −1.84c/book into +3.01c.
